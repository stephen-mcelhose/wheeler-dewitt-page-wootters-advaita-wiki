# The Problems of Time

The frozen-formalism issue associated with the [Wheeler–DeWitt Formalism](wheeler-dewitt-formalism.md) is only one facet of the problem of time. Other facets include the construction of observables, global-clock obstructions, inequivalent clock choices, [physical-inner-product](../definitions/def-physical-state.md) questions, [constraint anomalies](../definitions/def-anomaly.md), and spacetime reconstruction.

The phrase “timeless” must be handled carefully: constraint-generated parameter evolution can be gauge, while gauge-invariant relational correlations can still encode change. No single framework is accepted as solving every facet in full quantum gravity.
