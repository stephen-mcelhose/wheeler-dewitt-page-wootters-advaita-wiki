# Orthonormal basis

An orthonormal basis has vectors of unit norm with pairwise zero inner product, and its span is dense in the Hilbert space. For a discrete basis \(\{|n\rangle\}\), this is written \(\langle n|m\rangle=\delta_{nm}\).

Learn more: [Bases and Coordinates](../mathematics/bases-and-coordinates.md).
