# Clock Reading and External Time

A clock reading is a selected internal or operational outcome label, while external time is a parameter supplied by an ordinary external-time model. A clock reading, gauge parameter, and external time are distinct structures unless a model explicitly establishes a relation among them.

Learn more: [Clock POVMs and Operational Conditioning](../page-wootters/clock-povms.md).
