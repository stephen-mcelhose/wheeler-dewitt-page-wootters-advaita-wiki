# Coordinates relative to a basis

If \(B=(b_1,\ldots,b_n)\) is an ordered basis and \(v=\sum_jc_jb_j\), then \((c_1,\ldots,c_n)^T\) is the coordinate column of \(v\) relative to \(B\). Coordinates depend on the chosen ordered basis; they are not the abstract vector itself.

Learn more: [Bases and Coordinates](../mathematics/bases-and-coordinates.md).
