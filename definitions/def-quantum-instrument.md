# Quantum Instrument

A finite discrete instrument is a family \(\{\mathcal I_x\}_x\) of completely positive, trace-nonincreasing maps whose sum is trace preserving. It gives an unnormalized selective output and its probability for every outcome; when that probability is positive, it gives the corresponding normalized selective output. “Completely positive” is explained in the associated concept rather than assumed here.

Learn more: [Measurements and Conditional States](../mathematics/conditional-states.md).
