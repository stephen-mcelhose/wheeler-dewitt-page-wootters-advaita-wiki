# Resolution of the identity

For a discrete orthonormal basis, the resolution of the identity is \(\sum_n |n\rangle\langle n|=I\), with the sum understood in the appropriate convergence sense. It allows vectors to be expanded in that basis.

Learn more: [Dirac Notation](../mathematics/dirac-notation.md).
