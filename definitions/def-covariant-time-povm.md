# Covariant Time POVM

A covariant time POVM is a POVM on a time-outcome space whose effects or outcome probabilities transform covariantly under the relevant time-translation dynamics. It is an operational observable structure, not automatically a self-adjoint time operator.

Learn more: [Clock POVMs and Operational Conditioning](../page-wootters/clock-povms.md).
