# Conjugate Momentum

A conjugate momentum is the canonical variable paired with a configuration variable; for the ADM spatial metric, it is \(\pi^{ab}\) paired with \(h_{ab}\). Its canonical pairing determines the corresponding Poisson brackets.

Learn more: [Canonical Gravity and the ADM Split](../physics/canonical-gravity.md).
