# Basis expansion

For a discrete orthonormal basis, a vector has the expansion \(|\psi\rangle=\sum_n|n\rangle\langle n|\psi\rangle\), with appropriate convergence. The coefficients \(\langle n|\psi\rangle\) are its components in that basis.

Learn more: [Dirac Notation](../mathematics/dirac-notation.md).
