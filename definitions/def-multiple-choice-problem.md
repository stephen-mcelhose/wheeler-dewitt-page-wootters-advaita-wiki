# Multiple-Choice Problem

The multiple-choice problem is the possibility that different choices of internal clock or reduction can lead to inequivalent quantum descriptions or observables. It is not solved merely by selecting one clock variable.

Learn more: [Limits of the Page–Wootters Mechanism](../page-wootters/page-wootters-limitations.md).
