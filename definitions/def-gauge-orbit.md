# Gauge Orbit

A gauge orbit is the set of phase-space points connected by the relevant first-class gauge transformations. Its points are alternative descriptions of one physical situation within the stated regular local model, not successive instants of external time.

Learn more: [Classical Constrained Systems](../mathematics/constrained-systems.md).
