# Momentum Constraint

In ADM gravity, the momentum constraint is the local vector condition \(\mathcal H_a(x)\approx0\) on each spatial point. Its smeared form generates spatial diffeomorphisms of the canonical data in the declared classical regime.

Learn more: [Hamiltonian and Momentum Constraints](../physics/adm-constraints.md).
