# Discrete basis

A discrete basis is indexed by a discrete set, so expansions and resolutions of the identity are written as sums. Continuous-spectrum notation instead requires the qualifications explained in Spectra and Generalized Bases.

Learn more: [Bases and Coordinates](../mathematics/bases-and-coordinates.md).
