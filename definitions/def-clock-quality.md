# Clock Quality and Finite Resolution

Clock quality and finite resolution describe operational limits on a clock’s distinguishable readouts, duration, accuracy, and recurrence behavior in a finite model. They do not by themselves establish a canonical time operator or a full-gravity clock.

Learn more: [Finite Clock Models](../page-wootters/finite-clock-model.md).
