# Physical Inner Product

The physical inner product is the positive sesquilinear pairing on the physical state space that determines physical amplitudes and probabilities after the constraints are addressed. It need not be the restriction of the kinematical inner product, especially when solutions are distributional or gauge-equivalent.

Learn more: [Formal and Physical State Spaces](../mathematics/formal-and-physical-state-spaces.md).
