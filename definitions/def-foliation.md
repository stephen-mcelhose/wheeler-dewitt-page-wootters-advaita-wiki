# Spacetime Foliation

A family of spatial slices \(\Sigma_t\) that represents spacetime in a 3+1 decomposition. It is a formulation choice under stated assumptions, not a preferred physical time.

Learn more: [Spacetime Foliations and General Relativity Primer](../physics/spacetime-foliations-and-general-relativity-primer.md).
