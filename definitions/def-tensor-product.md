# Tensor Product

The tensor product \(\mathcal H_C\otimes\mathcal H_S\) combines specified subsystem spaces; a simple tensor \(|c\rangle\otimes|s\rangle\) specifies one vector from each factor. General composite vectors can be sums of simple tensors.

Learn more: [Composite Quantum Systems](../mathematics/composite-systems.md).
