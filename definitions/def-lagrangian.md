# Lagrangian

A Lagrangian is a function \(L(q,\dot q,t)\) of configuration coordinates, their externally timed velocities, and possibly time. In a regular classical model, the Euler–Lagrange equations determine the motion and the conjugate momenta are \(p_i=\partial L/\partial\dot q^i\). A singular velocity–momentum relation can instead lead to constraints.
