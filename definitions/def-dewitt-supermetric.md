# DeWitt Supermetric

The DeWitt supermetric is the indefinite bilinear form on spatial-metric variations; in the declared ADM convention, the lower-index \(G_{abcd}\) contracts \(\pi^{ab}\pi^{cd}\) in the scalar-constraint kinetic term. Its indefiniteness is one reason the Wheeler–DeWitt setting is not an ordinary positive-definite Schrödinger configuration space.
