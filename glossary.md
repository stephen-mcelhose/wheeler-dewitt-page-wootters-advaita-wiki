# Glossary

Definitions are short recall aids. Use the linked concept pages for first learning.

## Mathematical foundations

- [Inner product](definitions/def-inner-product.md) — A complex-valued rule that compares two vectors and induces length and orthogonality.
- [Inner-product space](mathematics/inner-product-spaces.md) — A vector space equipped with an inner product.
- [Norm](definitions/def-norm.md) — A vector length, here induced by the inner product.
- [Cauchy sequence](definitions/def-cauchy-sequence.md) — A sequence whose later terms become arbitrarily close to one another.
- [Completeness](definitions/def-completeness.md) — The property that Cauchy sequences converge inside the space.
- [Hilbert space](mathematics/hilbert-spaces.md) — A complete inner-product space.
- [Ray](definitions/def-ray.md) — A class of nonzero vectors representing the same pure physical state.
- [Normalization](definitions/def-normalization.md) — Choosing a unit-norm representative.
- [Orthogonality](definitions/def-orthogonality.md) — Zero inner product between vectors.
- [Dirac notation](mathematics/dirac-notation.md) — Bra–ket notation for vectors, dual vectors, overlaps, and operators.
- [Ket](definitions/def-ket.md) — A Dirac-notated vector \(|\psi\rangle\) in a Hilbert space.
- [Bra](definitions/def-bra.md) — The dual-vector functional \(\langle\psi|\) associated with a ket by the inner product.
- [Dual vector](definitions/def-dual-vector.md) — A continuous linear functional on a Hilbert space; the inner product identifies each vector with one.
- [Overlap](definitions/def-overlap.md) — The inner product \(\langle\phi|\psi\rangle\) of two kets.
- [Orthonormal basis](definitions/def-orthonormal-basis.md) — A complete orthogonal unit-vector basis.
- [Discrete basis](definitions/def-discrete-basis.md) — A basis indexed by a discrete set, written as a sum rather than a continuous-spectrum integral.
- [Kronecker delta](definitions/def-kronecker-delta.md) — \(\delta_{nm}=1\) when \(n=m\), and \(0\) otherwise.
- [Resolution of the identity](definitions/def-resolution-of-identity.md) — A convergent sum of basis projectors equal to the identity operator.
- [Basis expansion](definitions/def-basis-expansion.md) — The representation of a vector as a convergent sum of components along a basis.
- [Born rule](definitions/def-born-rule.md) — The measurement rule assigning probabilities from states and measurement operators.
- [Generalized basis](mathematics/spectral-notation.md) — Distributional notation for continuous spectra; it is not an ordinary normalized basis in the Hilbert space.
- [Self-adjoint operator](mathematics/operators-and-domains.md) — An operator whose domain and adjoint satisfy the condition needed for spectral calculus and unitary evolution.
- [POVM](mathematics/conditional-states.md) — A positive-operator-valued measure used to represent generalized measurements.
- [Conditional state](mathematics/conditional-states.md) — A state assigned relative to a specified measurement outcome or clock reading.
- [Dirac quantization](mathematics/dirac-quantization.md) — Quantization in which constraints are imposed on a kinematical representation and a physical state space is then constructed.
- [Lagrangian](definitions/def-lagrangian.md) — A function of coordinates, velocities, and possibly time that determines regular classical motion and conjugate momenta.

## Canonical gravity

- [ADM split](physics/canonical-gravity.md) — A foliation-based canonical formulation of general relativity using spatial geometry, lapse, and shift.
- [Lapse and shift](physics/canonical-gravity.md) — Multiplier fields that describe the chosen hypersurface deformation in an ADM representation.
- [Conjugate momentum](definitions/def-conjugate-momentum.md) — The canonical phase-space variable paired with a configuration variable.
- [Hamiltonian constraint](definitions/def-hamiltonian-constraint.md) — The local scalar ADM constraint \(\mathcal H_\perp(x)\approx0\).
- [Momentum constraint](definitions/def-momentum-constraint.md) — The local vector ADM constraint \(\mathcal H_a(x)\approx0\) generating spatial diffeomorphisms.
- [DeWitt supermetric](definitions/def-dewitt-supermetric.md) — The indefinite configuration-space metric entering the gravitational kinetic term.
- [Superspace](physics/superspace.md) — Formally, \(\mathrm{Riem}(\Sigma)/\mathrm{Diff}(\Sigma)\), the space of spatial geometries.
- [Minisuperspace](physics/superspace.md) — A symmetry-reduced model; it is not full canonical gravity.
- [Wheeler–DeWitt formalism](physics/wheeler-dewitt-formalism.md) — Formal local quantum constraint equations in canonical gravity.
- [Dirac observable](physics/relational-observables.md) — A gauge-invariant complete observable, defined relative to the constraints.

## Additional first-use terms

- [Vector Space](definitions/def-vector-space.md) — A collection of vectors closed under addition and scalar multiplication.
- [Generalized Eigenvector](definitions/def-generalized-eigenvector.md) — A distributional object satisfying a formal eigenvalue equation for a continuous spectrum; it need not be a Hilbert-space vector.
- [Rigged Hilbert Space](definitions/def-rigged-hilbert-space.md) — A triple \(\Phi\subset\mathcal H\subset\Phi\prime\) that places test vectors, a Hilbert space, and distributional dual objects in one framework.
- [Operator Domain](definitions/def-operator-domain.md) — The subset of a Hilbert space on which an operator is defined.
- [Adjoint](definitions/def-adjoint.md) — The operator defined by moving an inner product from one argument to the other, with a domain determined by that requirement.
- [Symmetric Operator](definitions/def-symmetric-operator.md) — An operator whose matrix-element symmetry holds on its domain; this alone does not make it self-adjoint.
- [Self-Adjoint Operator](definitions/def-self-adjoint-operator.md) — An operator equal to its adjoint with the same domain.
- [Commutator](definitions/def-commutator.md) — The domain-sensitive difference \([A,B]=AB-BA\) of two operators.
- [Tensor Product](definitions/def-tensor-product.md) — The Hilbert-space construction used to combine independently modeled subsystems.
- [Density Operator](definitions/def-density-operator.md) — A positive trace-one operator representing a quantum state, pure or mixed.
- [Trace](definitions/def-trace.md) — For a trace-class operator, the basis-independent sum of diagonal matrix elements.
- [Partial Trace](definitions/def-partial-trace.md) — The operation that discards one factor of a composite system to give an unconditioned reduced state.
- [POVM](definitions/def-povm.md) — A collection or measure of positive effects that sums or integrates to the identity and represents generalized outcomes.
- [Quantum Instrument](definitions/def-quantum-instrument.md) — A family of outcome maps that specifies probabilities and selective post-measurement states.
- [Measurement Effect](definitions/def-effect.md) — A positive operator bounded by the identity that supplies one generalized-measurement outcome probability.
- [Reduced State](definitions/def-reduced-state.md) — The unconditioned state of one factor after the other factor is discarded.
- [Conditional State](definitions/def-conditional-state.md) — A normalized state assigned relative to a declared condition with nonzero probability.
- [Pure-Bipartite Entanglement](definitions/def-entanglement.md) — For a specified bipartition, a pure state that cannot be written as one product of factor states.
- [Phase Space](definitions/def-phase-space.md) — The classical state space locally coordinatized by configurations and conjugate momenta.
- [Poisson Bracket](definitions/def-poisson-bracket.md) — The canonical classical bracket of phase-space functions.
- [Hamiltonian](definitions/def-hamiltonian.md) — A phase-space function generating flow in a stated canonical model.
- [Hamiltonian Flow](definitions/def-hamiltonian-flow.md) — The bracket-generated phase-space curve of a stated generator.
- [Constraint Surface](definitions/def-constraint-surface.md) — The subset where every specified constraint vanishes.
- [Canonical Hamiltonian](definitions/def-canonical-hamiltonian.md) — The Legendre-transform Hamiltonian before primary-constraint multipliers are added.
- [Total Hamiltonian](definitions/def-total-hamiltonian.md) — The canonical Hamiltonian plus primary constraints with multipliers.
- [Gauge Orbit](definitions/def-gauge-orbit.md) — Points related by the relevant first-class gauge transformations.
- [First-Class Constraint](definitions/def-first-class-constraint.md) — A constraint whose Poisson brackets with all constraints vanish on the constraint surface, under the relevant regularity assumptions.
- [Second-Class Constraint](definitions/def-second-class-constraint.md) — A constraint set whose mutual Poisson-bracket matrix is invertible on the constraint surface.
- [Weak Equality](definitions/def-weak-equality.md) — An equality imposed only after restricting to the constraint surface.
- [Constraint](definitions/def-constraint.md) — A phase-space condition that restricts physically allowed configurations or momenta.
- [Kinematical State](definitions/def-kinematical-state.md) — A state in the preliminary representation before quantum constraints and the physical inner product are fully imposed.
- [Physical State](definitions/def-physical-state.md) — A state satisfying the quantum constraints in the completed physical construction.
- [Physical Inner Product](definitions/def-physical-inner-product.md) — The inner product on a physical state space that fixes physical amplitudes and probabilities.
- [Factor Ordering](definitions/def-factor-ordering.md) — The choice of operator order when quantizing noncommuting classical factors.
- [Constraint Anomaly](definitions/def-anomaly.md) — A failure of quantum constraint commutators to realize the required classical constraint relations.
- [Induced Spatial Metric](definitions/def-induced-metric.md) — The metric on a spatial hypersurface inherited from the spacetime metric.
- [Lagrange Multiplier](definitions/def-lagrange-multiplier.md) — An auxiliary variable whose variation enforces a constraint.
- [Smearing](definitions/def-smearing.md) — Integration of a local constraint against a test function or vector field to form a well-defined generator.
- [Structure Function](definitions/def-structure-function.md) — A phase-space-dependent coefficient in a constraint bracket, unlike a constant structure coefficient.
- [Functional Derivative](definitions/def-functional-derivative.md) — The derivative of a functional with respect to a function or field configuration.
- [Deparametrization](definitions/def-deparametrization.md) — A reduction that solves a constraint using a chosen clock variable and yields evolution in that variable.
- [Partial Observable](definitions/def-partial-observable.md) — A quantity that can be specified or measured but is not necessarily gauge invariant by itself.
- [Complete Observable](definitions/def-complete-observable.md) — A gauge-invariant correlation that gives one quantity when another is assigned a clock value.
- [Clock/System Factorization](definitions/def-clock-system-factorization.md) — A chosen tensor-product decomposition that identifies degrees of freedom as clock and system.
- [Clock-System Backreaction](definitions/def-backreaction.md) — A change in clock or system behavior caused by their interaction.
- [Global Clock](definitions/def-global-clock.md) — A clock variable that is well defined and monotonic throughout the relevant physical domain.
- [Minisuperspace](definitions/def-minisuperspace.md) — A symmetry-reduced configuration-space model retaining only selected gravitational degrees of freedom.
