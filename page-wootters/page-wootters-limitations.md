# Limits of the Page–Wootters Mechanism

The [Page–Wootters mechanism](page-wootters-mechanism.md) has the following limits. Finite clocks have limited readout resolution and, in cyclic finite models, recurrence; any usable observation range is model-dependent. Clock–system interactions and possible [clock-system backreaction](../definitions/def-backreaction.md) are not resolved by the noninteracting additive model. The clock/system split can be nonunique, especially in constrained or gravitational settings, and interactions used to read a clock may spoil an ideal decomposition.

The ideal-clock derivation uses generalized clock vectors, compatible operator domains, differentiability, and a formal normalization prescription. A rigorous continuous-clock construction requires further test-space/measure and physical-inner-product work. The finite-clock and instrument descriptions likewise do not identify their selected constraint with full gravitational constraints.

Page–Wootters does not by itself construct the physical Hilbert space of full gravity, establish anomaly-free local constraints, guarantee a [global clock](../definitions/def-global-clock.md), solve the {{def:multiple-choice-problem|multiple-choice problem}}, or reconstruct spacetime and locality. These limits define its proper scope.
