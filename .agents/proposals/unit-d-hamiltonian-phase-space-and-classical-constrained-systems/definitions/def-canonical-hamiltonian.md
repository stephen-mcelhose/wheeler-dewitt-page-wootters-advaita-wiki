# Canonical Hamiltonian

The canonical Hamiltonian \(H_{\rm can}\) is obtained by the Legendre construction from a Lagrangian. In a singular theory it may be supplemented by primary constraints in the total Hamiltonian.

Learn more: [Classical Constrained Systems](../mathematics/constrained-systems.md).
