# Poisson Bracket

In local canonical coordinates, \(\{F,G\}=\sum_i(\partial F/\partial q^i\,\partial G/\partial p_i-\partial F/\partial p_i\,\partial G/\partial q^i)\). It is a classical operation: it is neither an inner product nor, without further quantization assumptions, a quantum commutator.
