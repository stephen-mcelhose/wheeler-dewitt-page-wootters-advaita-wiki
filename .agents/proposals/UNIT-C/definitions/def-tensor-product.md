# Tensor Product

The tensor product \(\mathcal H_C\otimes\mathcal H_S\) is the composite space built from specified factors \(C\) and \(S\). A simple tensor \(|c\rangle_C\otimes|s\rangle_S\) represents independently specified factor vectors; general composite vectors can be sums of simple tensors and need not be product states.

Learn more: [Composite Quantum Systems](../mathematics/composite-systems.md).
