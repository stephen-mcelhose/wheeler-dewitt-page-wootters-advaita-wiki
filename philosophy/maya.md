# Māyā

> [!WARNING] Under Construction
> **Not reviewed.** This is exploratory opinion and personal philosophical reflection, not a scientific or metaphysical conclusion.
> This draft page awaits primary-text and scholarly-source review. It is not a scientific interpretation of quantum theory or quantum gravity.

*Māyā* should not be reduced to the word “illusion.” In Advaita interpretation, it is connected with the experienced world, *avidyā* (ignorance),, ignorance, and the maintenance of ultimate non-duality; its precise status and role vary across texts and scholarly interpretations.

It is not a scientific claim about quantum states, measurement, spacetime, or the Wheeler–DeWitt equation.
