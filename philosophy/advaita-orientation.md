# Optional Philosophical Orientation: [Advaita Vedānta](https://iep.utm.edu/adv-veda/)

> [!WARNING] Under Construction
> **Not reviewed.** This is exploratory opinion and personal philosophical reflection, not a scientific or metaphysical conclusion.
> This optional philosophical layer is still a draft and does not yet meet the wiki's planned primary-text and scholarly-source coverage. It must not be used to draw conclusions about the physics curriculum.

This optional section begins only after the physics curriculum. Advaita Vedānta is a philosophical and interpretive tradition associated especially with Śaṅkara, with ultimate non-dual reality discussed as *Brahman* (the tradition's term for ultimate reality). Its questions, methods, and standards of support differ from those of mathematical physics.

No physical formalism in this wiki is presented as evidence for an Advaita doctrine, and no Advaita doctrine is presented as a derivation of a physical formalism.
