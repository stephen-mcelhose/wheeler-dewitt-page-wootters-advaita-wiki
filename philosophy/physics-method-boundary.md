# Physics–Philosophy Method Boundary

> [!WARNING] Under Construction
> **Not reviewed.** This is exploratory opinion and personal philosophical reflection, not a scientific or metaphysical conclusion.
> This draft boundary page will be expanded after the philosophy sources are strengthened. It defines a personal, exploratory mathematical-modeling space; it does not license science–philosophy conflation.

Physics evaluates formalisms through mathematical definition, empirical connection, and scientific criticism. [Advaita](advaita-orientation.md) and broader non-duality traditions are assessed through textual interpretation, philosophical argument, and their own intellectual traditions.

## Required four-part structure for exploratory models

1. **Established statement** — state the standard mathematics or physics first.
2. **Proposed analogy/model** — name the comparison as a personal exploratory construct.
3. **Interpretive reflection** — describe the question or perspective the model invites.
4. **What does not follow** — state the non-claims explicitly.

The exploratory section may deliberately label vectors **Alice**, **Bob**, or **JON**. Those labels are pedagogical names, not claims that vectors are people. A bra, ket, [inner product](../definitions/def-inner-product.md), or [projector](../definitions/def-projector.md) may be a bounded metaphor only after its conventional definition is stated.

A comparison may ask whether both domains challenge a naive image of time as an independently given background. It may not claim that Page–Wootters proves māyā, that māyā predicts a quantum-gravity equation, that a projector models consciousness, or that the concepts are identical.
